# HoundRelay privacy policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

HoundRelay is an independent Connect IQ app. It is not made, supported or
approved by Garmin.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. HoundRelay has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or
developer-operated servers. The developer does not receive your location,
settings or any other data from the app.

## What the app processes on your watch

- Dog position data received from your handheld.
- Your watch's GPS position. This is on by default and can be turned off in
  the app settings.
- A randomly generated installation identifier. It is not your name, account
  or device serial number.
- The receiver address and credentials you enter in the app settings.
- Observations waiting to be uploaded, kept in bounded app storage on the
  watch.

## Where data is sent

Only to the receiving server you configure, over HTTPS, through your phone's
Garmin Connect app. How that server stores or uses the data is decided by
whoever operates it, under their own privacy policy.

## Your control

- Relay does not start automatically unless you enable it.
- You can pause relay, change or remove the receiver, or turn off watch GPS in
  the app settings.
- Uninstalling the app removes it from your watch.
